    Turn and game

    Starting a game
    20 life each, shuffle, draw 7. A random player picks who goes first. Commander starts at 40 life. wiki
    Mulligan
    Don't like your 7? Shuffle and draw 7 again, then put one card on the bottom for each mulligan taken. In multiplayer the first mulligan is free. wiki
    Turn order
    Untap, upkeep, draw. Main phase 1. Combat. Main phase 2. End step, cleanup. The player who goes first skips their first draw. wiki
    Untap step
    Untap all your permanents. Nobody can cast anything here. wiki
    Upkeep
    Start-of-turn step after untap. "At the beginning of your upkeep" triggers happen here. You can cast instants. wiki
    Land per turn
    One land per turn, in your main phase, with the stack empty. Playing a land does not use the stack, so it can't be responded to. wiki
    Summoning sickness
    A creature can't attack or use {T} abilities unless you've controlled it since the start of your turn. Haste removes this. wiki
    End step
    "At the beginning of the end step" triggers happen here. Last normal chance to cast instants on this turn. wiki
    Cleanup
    Discard down to 7 cards, then damage on creatures is removed and "until end of turn" effects end. wiki
    How you lose
    0 or less life. Drawing from an empty library. 10 poison counters. In Commander, also 21 combat damage from one commander. wiki
    Both hit 0 at once
    If every remaining player loses at the same time, the game is a draw. wiki
    Passing the turn
    Saying "go" or "pass" means you're done with your main phase and want to move to your end step. wiki
    Draw step
    Draw a card at the start of it. Drawing from an empty library makes you lose the game. wiki
    Main phases
    Two per turn, before and after combat. Creatures, sorceries and your land only go there, with the stack empty. wiki
    Extra turns
    Taken right after the current turn. If several are made, the newest one happens first. wiki
    Conceding
    You can concede at any time. In multiplayer the game goes on without you. wiki

    Stack and timing

    The stack
    Spells and abilities wait on the stack. Players can respond. The last one added resolves first. wiki
    Priority
    The top of the stack resolves only after both players pass in a row without adding anything. wiki
    Responding
    "In response" means adding your instant or ability on top, so it resolves before the thing you responded to. wiki
    Sorcery speed vs instant speed
    Sorceries and permanents: your main phase, empty stack. Instants and flash cards: any time you have priority. wiki
    Opponent's turn
    You can cast instants, flash cards and activate abilities on their turn. Sorceries and normal creatures: only on yours. wiki
    Activated ability
    Written "Cost: Effect". You choose when to use it. Uses the stack (mana abilities don't). wiki
    Triggered ability
    Starts with "When", "Whenever" or "At". Goes on the stack by itself when the event happens. wiki
    Static ability
    Always on while the card is out. No stack, nothing to respond to. wiki
    Mana ability
    Adds mana and has no target, like tapping a land. Doesn't use the stack, can't be responded to. wiki
    Costs can't be responded to
    Things paid as a cost (tapping, sacrificing, discarding) happen immediately. Only the effect goes on the stack. wiki
    Counter
    A countered spell does nothing and goes to the graveyard. Lands can't be countered because they aren't spells. wiki
    Fizzle
    If every target is gone or illegal when a spell resolves, it does nothing. wiki
    Split second
    While it's on the stack, nobody can cast spells or activate non-mana abilities. Triggers still happen. wiki
    State-based actions
    Checked all the time: 0 life loses, creatures with lethal damage or 0 toughness die, legend rule applies. wiki
    Cast vs put onto the battlefield
    "Put onto the battlefield" is not casting: it can't be countered and "whenever you cast" doesn't trigger. wiki
    Copy of a spell
    A copy is not cast, so "whenever you cast" doesn't trigger. It goes on the stack and resolves like the original. wiki

    Combat

    Combat steps
    Beginning of combat, declare attackers, declare blockers, combat damage, end of combat. wiki
    Attacking
    Attackers tap (unless vigilance). Each one attacks a player, a planeswalker or a battle, your choice. wiki
    Blocking
    Each untapped creature blocks at most one attacker. Several can block the same attacker; its controller splits the damage between them. wiki
    Unblocked
    An unblocked attacker deals its damage to whatever it attacked. wiki
    Lethal damage
    A creature dies once its damage this turn reaches its toughness. wiki
    Chump block
    Blocking with a creature that will die without killing the attacker, just to save life. wiki
    Trade
    Two creatures block or fight and both die.
    Combat trick
    An instant cast during combat, usually after blocks, like a pump spell, to win a fight. wiki
    Attacker removed after blocks
    The other creature stays in combat. A blocked attacker stays blocked and deals no damage unless it has trample. wiki
    Tapping a blocker
    Tapping a creature after it blocked doesn't remove it from combat. It still deals damage. wiki
    First strike step
    If any creature has first strike or double strike, combat damage happens in two steps. wiki
    Planeswalker damage
    Damage to a planeswalker removes that many loyalty counters. At 0 loyalty it dies. wiki
    Defending player
    The player being attacked. In multiplayer, each attacker can attack a different player. wiki
    Several blockers
    More than one creature can block the same attacker. The attacker's controller splits its damage among them however they like. wiki
    Damage is simultaneous
    All combat damage is dealt at the same time, unless first strike or double strike adds an earlier damage step. wiki

    Keywords

    Trample
    Damage beyond what kills the blockers goes to the player. With deathtouch, 1 damage per blocker is enough. wiki
    Deathtouch
    Any damage it deals to a creature destroys that creature. wiki
    Flying
    Can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach. wiki
    Reach
    Can block creatures with flying. wiki
    First strike
    Deals combat damage before creatures without it. wiki
    Double strike
    Deals combat damage twice: in the first-strike step and the normal one. wiki
    Lifelink
    Damage it deals also gains you that much life. wiki
    Vigilance
    Doesn't tap to attack, so it can still block next turn. wiki
    Haste
    Can attack and use {T} abilities the turn it comes in. wiki
    Flash
    You can cast it any time you could cast an instant. wiki
    Menace
    Can't be blocked except by two or more creatures. wiki
    Defender
    Can't attack. wiki
    Hexproof
    Your opponents' spells and abilities can't target it. Yours can. wiki
    Shroud
    Nobody's spells or abilities can target it, not even yours. wiki
    Ward
    When an opponent targets it, their spell or ability is countered unless they pay the ward cost. wiki
    Indestructible
    Damage and "destroy" don't kill it. Exile, sacrifice and 0 toughness still do. wiki
    Protection
    Protection from X: can't be damaged, enchanted or equipped, blocked or targeted by X. Remember DEBT. wiki
    Prowess
    Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, it gets +1/+1 until end of turn. wiki
    Equip
    Pay the cost to attach the Equipment to a creature you control. Sorcery speed only. If the creature leaves, the Equipment stays on the battlefield, unattached. wiki
    Enchant
    An Aura's "Enchant creature" says what it can be attached to. If that thing leaves, the Aura goes to the graveyard. wiki
    Cycling
    Pay the cycling cost and discard the card from your hand: draw a card. Instant speed. wiki
    Kicker
    Optional extra cost when casting, for a bigger effect. wiki
    Flashback
    Cast it from your graveyard for the flashback cost. It's exiled afterwards. wiki
    Convoke
    Each creature you tap while casting pays for {1} or one mana of that creature's color. wiki
    Delve
    Exile cards from your graveyard while casting. Each one pays {1}. wiki
    Cascade
    When you cast it, exile cards from the top until a nonland card with lower mana value. You may cast that free. The rest go to the bottom. wiki
    Landfall
    Triggers whenever a land enters under your control. wiki
    Infect
    Deals damage to creatures as -1/-1 counters and to players as poison counters. wiki
    Toxic
    Toxic N: combat damage to a player also gives them N poison counters. 10 poison loses. wiki
    Persist
    When it dies, if it had no -1/-1 counters, it returns with a -1/-1 counter. wiki
    Undying
    When it dies, if it had no +1/+1 counters, it returns with a +1/+1 counter. wiki
    Crew
    Crew N: tap creatures with total power N or more to make the Vehicle a creature until end of turn. New creatures can crew. wiki
    Storm
    When you cast it, copy it once for each other spell cast before it this turn. wiki
    Affinity for artifacts
    Costs {1} less for each artifact you control. wiki

    Keyword actions

    Destroy
    Put it into its owner's graveyard. Indestructible stops this. Regenerate can replace it. wiki
    Exile
    Remove it from the game into the exile zone. Doesn't count as dying. wiki
    Sacrifice
    Move your own permanent to the graveyard. You can only sacrifice your own. Indestructible doesn't help. wiki
    Bounce (return to hand)
    Return a permanent to its owner's hand. Tokens bounced this way stop existing. wiki
    Fight
    Each creature deals damage equal to its power to the other. wiki
    Scry
    Scry N: look at the top N cards, put any on the bottom and the rest back on top in any order. wiki
    Surveil
    Surveil N: look at the top N cards, put any into your graveyard and the rest back on top in any order. wiki
    Mill
    Mill N: put the top N cards of the library into the graveyard. wiki
    Discard
    Put a card from hand into the graveyard. "Random" means you don't pick. wiki
    Search (tutor)
    Look through your library for a card, then shuffle. You may fail to find if it asks for a quality like "creature". wiki
    Proliferate
    Choose any permanents and players with counters, give each another counter of a kind it already has. wiki
    Investigate
    Create a Clue token. wiki
    Transform
    Turn a double-faced card to its other face. It stays the same object, not a new one. wiki
    Create
    Make a token. The card tells you its name, type and stats. wiki

    Card types

    Creature
    Has power/toughness, can attack and block. Cast at sorcery speed. wiki
    Instant
    A one-shot spell you can cast any time you have priority. wiki
    Sorcery
    A one-shot spell cast only in your main phase with the stack empty. wiki
    Artifact
    A permanent. Colorless unless its cost has colored mana. wiki
    Enchantment
    A permanent with an ongoing effect. wiki
    Aura
    An enchantment that attaches to something. It targets when cast. wiki
    Equipment
    An artifact you attach to your creatures with equip. wiki
    Vehicle
    An artifact that becomes a creature when crewed. wiki
    Land
    Plays without the stack, once per turn. Usually taps for mana. Not a spell. wiki
    Basic land
    Plains, Island, Swamp, Mountain, Forest (and Wastes). Any number allowed in a deck. wiki
    Planeswalker
    Enters with loyalty. Use one loyalty ability per turn, at sorcery speed. Can be attacked. wiki
    Battle
    You cast a Siege, an opponent protects it. Attack it; when the last defense counter is gone, you exile it and may cast its back face free. wiki
    Saga
    Enchantment that gets a lore counter when it enters and after your draw step. Each chapter triggers. Sacrificed after the last one. wiki
    Legendary
    Only one with the same name per player on the battlefield (legend rule). wiki
    Kindred (tribal)
    A type that lets a non-creature card have creature types, like Kindred Instant - Elf. wiki
    Token
    A permanent that isn't a card. It stops existing once it leaves the battlefield. wiki
    Treasure
    Artifact token: {T}, sacrifice it: add one mana of any color. wiki
    Food
    Artifact token: {2}, {T}, sacrifice it: gain 3 life. wiki
    Clue
    Artifact token: {2}, sacrifice it: draw a card. wiki
    Double-faced card
    Transforming ones enter front face up and flip later. Modal ones (MDFC) let you pick which face to play. wiki
    Adventure
    Cast the adventure half first. It goes to exile, then you can cast the creature from exile later. wiki
    Split card
    Two spells on one card. Cast either half (or both with fuse). wiki

    Cards and zones

    Zones
    Library, hand, battlefield, graveyard, stack, exile, command. wiki
    Permanent
    Anything on the battlefield: creatures, artifacts, enchantments, lands, planeswalkers, battles. wiki
    Enters (ETB)
    "When this enters" triggers each time it arrives on the battlefield, cast or not. wiki
    Leaves (LTB)
    Triggers when it leaves the battlefield for any zone. wiki
    Dies
    "Dies" means put into a graveyard from the battlefield. Exile is not dying. wiki
    Owner vs controller
    The owner started the game with the card. Cards always go to their owner's hand, library or graveyard. wiki
    Legend rule
    If you control two legendary permanents with the same name, you keep one and put the other in the graveyard. wiki
    Tapped
    Turned sideways. Tapped creatures can't attack or block, and you can't use {T} abilities. wiki
    Counters on permanents
    +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters cancel each other out in pairs. wiki
    Graveyard
    Public. Anyone can look through anyone's graveyard any time. wiki
    Library
    Hidden, and you can't change its order unless a card says so. You can count how many cards are left. wiki
    Exile zone
    Usually face up and public. Cards there come back only if an effect says so. wiki
    Damage vs life loss
    "Lose life" isn't damage. Lifelink, prevention and protection only care about damage. wiki
    Mana burn
    Doesn't exist anymore. Unused mana just empties. wiki

    Mana and color

    Colors
    White, blue, black, red, green, written W U B R G. Colorless is not a color. wiki
    Mana value
    Total of the mana cost: {2}{G}{G} is 4. X counts as 0 except on the stack. Old name: converted mana cost (CMC). wiki
    Generic mana
    A number in a cost, like {3}. Any mana pays it. wiki
    Colorless mana
    The {C} symbol. Only colorless mana pays it, not colored. wiki
    Hybrid mana
    {W/U} is paid with either color. {2/W} is two generic or one white. wiki
    Phyrexian mana
    {W/P} is paid with white mana or 2 life. wiki
    X costs
    You choose X when you cast it and pay that much extra. wiki
    Mana pool
    Unused mana empties at the end of every step and phase. wiki
    Color identity
    All mana symbols in a card's cost and rules text. Commander uses it for deckbuilding. wiki
    Devotion
    Count of a color's mana symbols in the costs of permanents you control. wiki
    Guild and color pair names
    Azorius WU, Dimir UB, Rakdos BR, Gruul RG, Selesnya GW, Orzhov WB, Izzet UR, Golgari BG, Boros RW, Simic GU. wiki
    Multicolored
    A card with two or more colors, also called gold. wiki
    Mana fixing
    Lands and spells that make more than one color, so a multicolor deck can cast its cards. wiki
    Dual lands
    Lands that tap for two colors. Most enter tapped or cost life to stay fair. wiki
    Snow mana
    {S} is paid with mana made by a snow permanent, usually a snow land. wiki
    Hybrid in Commander
    A hybrid symbol counts as both colors for color identity, so {R/W} needs a commander with red and white. wiki

    Formats

    Standard
    Recent sets only. Older sets rotate out over time. wiki
    Pioneer
    Return to Ravnica (2012) onward. Doesn't rotate. wiki
    Modern
    Eighth Edition (2003) onward. Doesn't rotate. wiki
    Legacy
    Almost every card ever, with a ban list. wiki
    Vintage
    Every card. The strongest are restricted to one copy instead of banned. wiki
    Pauper
    Commons only. wiki
    Commander (EDH)
    Multiplayer, 100-card singleton deck led by a legendary creature, 40 life. wiki
    Draft
    Players open packs, pick one card and pass, then build 40-card decks from their picks. wiki
    Sealed
    Open six packs and build a 40-card deck from them. wiki
    Limited vs constructed
    Limited: deck built from packs opened at the event. Constructed: deck built beforehand from any legal cards. wiki
    Banned and restricted
    Banned cards can't be in the deck at all. Restricted (Vintage) means one copy max. wiki
    Rotation
    Once a year the oldest sets leave Standard as new ones arrive. wiki
    Brawl
    Commander-style singleton format played mostly on MTG Arena, with a smaller card pool. wiki
    Alchemy and Timeless
    MTG Arena formats. Alchemy adds digital-only and rebalanced cards. Timeless allows nearly every Arena card. wiki
    Cube
    A hand-picked set of cards, usually one of each, that a group drafts instead of booster packs. wiki
    Two-Headed Giant
    Teams of two share 30 life and take their turns together. wiki

    Commander

    Deck size
    Exactly 100 cards including the commander. Only one copy of each card except basic lands. wiki
    Who can be the commander
    A legendary creature, or a card that says it can be your commander. wiki
    Command zone
    Your commander starts there and you can cast it from there. wiki
    Commander tax
    Each time you cast it from the command zone, it costs {2} more than the last time. wiki
    Commander dies or is exiled
    You may move it back to the command zone instead. wiki
    Commander damage
    21 combat damage from the same commander makes a player lose. wiki
    Color identity rule
    Every card in the deck must fit inside your commander's color identity. wiki
    Partner and Background
    Lets you run two commanders. Deck is still 100 total. wiki
    Rule zero
    Talk before the game about how strong your decks are and any house rules. wiki
    Brackets
    Five power levels so decks at a table match: 1 Exhibition, 2 Core, 3 Upgraded, 4 Optimized, 5 cEDH. Still in beta. rules
    Game Changers
    A Wizards list of very strong cards. Brackets 1-2: none. Bracket 3: up to 3. Brackets 4-5: any number. Card lookup shows if a card is one. rules
    Bracket limits
    No mass land denial in brackets 1-3. No two-card infinite combos in 1-2, none that win early in 3. Extra turns: none in 1, a few never chained in 2-3. Brackets 4-5: anything not banned. rules
    Mass land denial
    Cards that destroy, exile, bounce or lock down lots of lands at once, like Armageddon, Winter Orb or Blood Moon. rules
    Commander banned list
    Cards nobody can play in Commander, at any bracket. Card lookup shows if a card is banned. rules

    Deckbuilding

    Constructed deck size
    At least 60 cards. At most 4 copies of a card by name, except basic lands. wiki
    Sideboard
    Up to 15 extra cards you can swap in between games of a match. wiki
    Limited deck size
    At least 40 cards. Add as many basic lands as you like. wiki
    How many lands
    About 24 in 60 cards, 17 in 40 cards, 36 to 38 in Commander. Fewer if your deck is cheap. wiki
    Mana curve
    How many cards you have at each mana value. Most decks want a lot of 2s and 3s. wiki
    Card advantage
    Having more useful cards than your opponent. Draw spells and 2-for-1s get it. wiki
    Tempo
    Using your mana and turns more efficiently than the opponent. wiki
    Archetypes
    Aggro attacks early, control stops things and wins late, midrange is in between, combo wins with a card combination. wiki
    Removal
    Cards that deal with an opponent's threats: destroy, exile, damage, bounce. wiki
    Ramp
    Getting more mana faster than normal: extra lands, mana creatures, Treasure. wiki
    Win condition
    The card or plan that actually ends the game. wiki
    Singleton
    Only one copy of each card except basic lands. Commander and most cubes are singleton. wiki
    Synergy
    Cards that are stronger together than on their own. wiki
    Splash
    Adding a few cards of an extra color, with some fixing to cast them. wiki
    Goldfishing
    Playing a deck alone for a few test games to see how it draws and curves out. wiki

    Slang

    ETB / LTB
    Enters the battlefield / leaves the battlefield. wiki
    EOT
    End of turn. "EOT, I cast this" means during the opponent's end step. wiki
    CMC / MV
    Converted mana cost, the old name of mana value. wiki
    Mana dork
    A cheap creature that taps for mana. wiki
    Wrath / board wipe
    A spell that destroys all creatures, named after Wrath of God. wiki
    Burn
    Direct damage spells, mostly red. wiki
    Cantrip
    A spell that also draws a card. wiki
    Bomb
    A single card that wins games on its own, mostly in Limited. wiki
    Staple
    A card played in many decks of a format.
    Mana screw / flood
    Screw: not enough lands. Flood: too many lands, not enough spells. wiki
    Topdeck
    The card you just drew, or winning off one. wiki
    Two-for-one
    One of your cards dealing with two of theirs. wiki
    Holding up mana
    Leaving lands untapped on your turn to cast instants on theirs. wiki
    Pump spell
    A spell that gives a creature +X/+X. wiki
    Pinger
    A creature that taps to deal 1 damage. wiki
    Proxy
    A stand-in card for a real one. Fine in casual play, not in sanctioned events. wiki

    Advanced rules

    Layers
    Ongoing effects apply in a fixed order: copy, control, text, type, color, abilities, then power/toughness. wiki
    Power/toughness order
    Inside the last layer: abilities that define P/T, then effects that set it, then +N/+N changes and counters, then switching. wiki
    Timestamps
    Inside one layer, older effects apply first and newer ones win. wiki
    Dependency
    If one effect changes what another applies to or does, the other waits for it, whatever the timestamps. wiki
    Replacement effect
    Uses "instead", "skip" or "enters with". Changes the event as it happens. No stack, nothing to respond to. wiki
    Several replacements at once
    The affected player, or the controller of the affected object, picks which one applies first. wiki
    Prevention effect
    "Prevent the next N damage" is a kind of replacement effect. It only stops damage, not life loss. wiki
    APNAP trigger order
    When several players' triggers happen at once, the active player stacks theirs first, then the others in turn order. So the active player's resolve last. wiki
    Ordering your triggers
    If several of your abilities trigger at once, you choose their order on the stack. wiki
    Intervening if
    "When X, if Y" checks Y when it triggers and again when it resolves. If Y is false either time, nothing happens. wiki
    Last known information
    If a source has left the battlefield, its ability uses how the source looked the moment it left.
    What a copy gets
    The printed card plus other copy effects. Not counters, Auras, +N/+N effects or tapped status. wiki
    Characteristic-defining ability
    Things like "its power is equal to...". Works in every zone, applied first in its layer. wiki
    Can't beats can
    If one effect says something can happen and another says it can't, it can't.
    Gaining control
    A creature you just took can't attack or tap for {T} this turn unless it has haste. wiki
    Casting steps
    Announce, choose modes and X, choose targets, work out the total cost, make mana, pay. Only then is it cast. wiki
    Alternative costs
    Flashback, evoke, "without paying its mana cost" and so on. Only one per spell. X is 0 when cast free. wiki
    Additional costs
    Kicker and similar. Can be paid on top of an alternative cost. wiki
    Regenerate
    The next time it would be destroyed this turn, instead tap it, remove its damage and take it out of combat. wiki
    Phasing
    A phased-out permanent is treated as if it doesn't exist. It phases back in on its controller's next untap step. No enter or leave triggers. wiki
    Face-down
    A face-down spell or permanent is a 2/2 colorless creature with no name, text or cost. Its controller can look at it. wiki
    Loops
    An optional loop: say how many times you repeat it. A loop nobody can stop is a draw. wiki
    Commander changing zones
    Going to graveyard or exile, it can move to the command zone right after, so "dies" still triggers. Hand or library: it's replaced instead. wiki
